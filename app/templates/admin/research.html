Overbooking: what the simulation found

A simulated half-day session for one doctor, with consultation times from the Hangu data (a clinic in China) and no-show risk from the model above (Kaggle, Brazil). Clinic behaviour is assumed: one doctor, patients who arrive on time and are seen in slot order, and a no-show uses no doctor time. This shows a method and its trade-offs, not what would happen in a real clinic.

P2 doubles the slots whose booked patient has a predicted risk above a threshold (a number such as 0.3, or a percentile such as p90 of the predicted risks). More patients served always costs waiting and overtime, so no single score is computed. Source: results/s2_policy_tradeoffs.csv.
